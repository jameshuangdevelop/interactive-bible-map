# Attribution

Every upstream source, dataset and image collection the project uses, with its license and the attribution it requires. Maintained by the Fact-Checker & Licensing agent. Individual images carry their own author, license and source in `data/media/`, and the app shows them. License decisions and compatibility reasoning are in [docs/LICENSES.md](docs/LICENSES.md); verification evidence is in [docs/verification/M1.md](docs/verification/M1.md), for the basemap in [docs/verification/M3-basemap.md](docs/verification/M3-basemap.md), for the ancient layer in [docs/verification/M4-ancient-geometry.md](docs/verification/M4-ancient-geometry.md), for the modern map in [docs/verification/M4-04-modern-map.md](docs/verification/M4-04-modern-map.md), for Paul's journeys in [docs/verification/M5-paul-journeys.md](docs/verification/M5-paul-journeys.md), and for the routes' lines in [docs/verification/M5-route-lines.md](docs/verification/M5-route-lines.md).
