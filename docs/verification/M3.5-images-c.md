# M3.5-04 verification — Images, batch C: the churches of Revelation

Independent verification of the Research Lead's briefs (`9fa4f35`, "docs(content): add research briefs for images batch C") and the Media Curator's images and prompts (`bb7b40c` and `5e3549c`) for Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea. Checked against the card (`docs/tasks/M3.5-04-batch-c-revelation-churches.md`, which follows `M3.5-02`), ADR-0017, ADR-0020, ADR-0026, ADR-0029 and `docs/LICENSES.md`. The branch tip before this review was `6925a4e`. Reviewed 2026-10-01.

**Result: pass after fixes, with no open findings.** All 39 images had an accepted license that matches Commons' `LicenseShortName` exactly. They all load, and all show the right place. But 24 image entries needed changes:

- 3 images were removed: a near-duplicate, a "few stones" view, and a roofless schematic model;
- 3 `kind` values were wrong;
- 1 author was incomplete;
- 21 captions on kept images were wrong or unsupported.

The briefs had 42 findings. The most serious were:

- **Laodicea's dates were wrong.** The brief put the stadium and the main colonnades after AD 95, but the excavators' own pages (the brief's cited source) date the stadium to AD 79 and the gates and main streets to AD 84–85.
- **Smyrna's streets were misread.** The brief turned Strabo's "large quadrangular porticoes" into colonnade-lined streets, and dated the "Golden Street" to the second century with no source.
- **Two non-WEB quotations:** Revelation 3:12 was quoted in the KJV's wording, and Colossians 4:15's name was given as "Nympha".
- **Uncited claims:** the Hadrianic round temple at Pergamon and the "steepest theatre" were not in the cited sources.

I fixed all of these myself, using the sources the briefs cite. I also rewrote 12 of the 15 prompts to match. The PO has since decided the one question this review raised (how to label historical views); see "For the PO".

## Method

1. **Sources opened.** These are the only sources the briefs cite. I read each one for the clauses it supports:
   - **Strabo, Loeb tr. Jones, LacusCurtius:**
     - 12.8.16–20 (Laodicea, the Lycus, the medical school, the Katakekaumene);
     - 13.4.1–14 (Pergamon, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, the Katakekaumene, the water at Laodicea);
     - 14.1.37 (Smyrna).
   - **Tacitus, *Annals*, Loeb tr. Jackson, LacusCurtius:** 2.47 (the earthquake of AD 17), 4.15 and 4.55–56 (the temple to Tiberius), 14.27 (Laodicea, AD 60).
   - **Ramsay, *The Letters to the Seven Churches* (1904):** the full archive.org text. I read the chapters on the six cities, and on the Jews and the imperial cult.
   - **The Archaeological Exploration of Sardis:** "About Sardis", "The Temple of Artemis" and "The Bath-Gymnasium Complex".
   - **Pamukkale University's Laodicea excavation:** the home page, *Tarihçe* (history), *Stadyum*, *Suriye Caddesi* and *Batı Tiyatrosu*. These pages are in Turkish, and I read them in Turkish.
   - **World History Encyclopedia:** "Pergamon".
   - **UNESCO's Pergamon listing:** it returned HTTP 403 to every request. I re-cited its two clauses with World History Encyclopedia, which states them, and kept the UNESCO citation beside it.
2. **Scripture.** I checked every quoted verse against the WEB snapshot (`data/reference/engwebp_vpl.txt`).
3. **Images.** I queried the Commons API (`imageinfo|categories`, `iiprop=url|size|extmetadata`) for all 39 files, in batches of 8 titles, with a descriptive User-Agent. I read the wikitext of the five files whose metadata raised questions. I rendered contact sheets with `sheet.js` and looked at every image. Then I downloaded 960 px or 500 px thumbnails of 12 images for a closer look.
4. **A replacement for Philadelphia's 1880s photograph.** I listed `Category:Alaşehir` (37 files) and its subcategories, and ran a Commons search for present-day views. There is no freely licensed wide view of today's town.
5. **Checks.** I ran `npm run validate:data` (0 errors), `npm test` (93/93) and `npm run check:images` (745 URLs: originals plus the 330, 500, 960 and 1280 px sizes). Every check passed after my changes.
