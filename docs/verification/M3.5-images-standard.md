# M3.5-05 verification: images for standard places

Independent check of the Media Curator's commit `50fa84c` ("data(media): improve images for standard places") and of every other image on the 31 standard places (`prominence: "standard"`), against ADR-0029 (the overall-feel rule, the image kinds and the gallery order), `docs/LICENSES.md` and the PO's review of the contact sheets. Reviewed 2026-10-01. The 31 major places were not touched.

## Method

- **License and credit.** For every image, read Commons' own file page through the API (`imageinfo` with `extmetadata`, and the wikitext where the credit was unclear). The `license` string was compared with Commons' `LicenseShortName` character for character, and the size, `author` and `sourcePage` against the same record. All licenses are public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, which `docs/LICENSES.md` accepts.
- **Subject.** Checked each file's Commons categories and description, not just its file name. The table shows the most specific category.
- **Looked at every image** as a 500 px thumbnail, on contact sheets for the existing images and one at a time for each new one.
- **Kind and caption.** `modern` for today's places and later buildings; `site` for excavated ancient remains; `historical` for old photographs and engravings of a place as it was when they were made; `reconstruction` only for depictions of the ancient place. Captions were checked for accuracy and neutrality, especially where a site is disputed (Derbe, Sychar).
- **Rules.** Ids follow display order, leads between about 4:3 and 2.2:1, every image at least 1,200 px wide, plain `upload.wikimedia.org` URLs. A script checked all four for all 62 images.

## Summary

- **62 images in the final data, all pass.** 68 files checked in all: the 62 that were in the data, plus six new ones.
- **PO flags a–g are all resolved:**
  - **Kinds fixed:** a (`nain-02` → `historical`) and b (`berea-02` → `modern`, with a factual caption).
  - **Replaced:** c (`samaria-01`), d (`nicopolis-01`), f (`iconium-02`, whose caption also named the wrong building) and g (`galilee-01`).
  - **e:** the courtyard image `sychar-02` was dropped, and a landscape exterior of the Jacob's Well church became the new lead.
- **Other fixes:**
  - **Kinds:** one more old engraving moved from `reconstruction` to `historical` (`derbe-01`), two Kavala images moved from `site` to `modern`, and the ruined harbour tower at Cenchreae moved from `modern` to `site`.
  - **Wrong caption:** `tarsus-01` said "city view" but shows the Roman road. It is now captioned as the road, and the duplicate road photo was dropped.
  - **Landscape lead:** `pool-of-bethesda` now opens with a landscape view instead of a portrait one.
  - **Small fixes:** one caption detail (`caesarea-philippi-01`) and one author credit (`chorazin-01`).
- **No license problems found.** Every `license` string matches Commons exactly.

## Neutrality notes

- **Derbe.** The record lists Kerti Höyük (high confidence) and Devri Şehri (low). The 1879 engraving is older than the 1956 inscription find, so the caption says it shows "the place E. J. Davis took to be the site of Derbe" and doesn't tie it to either candidate.
- **Sychar.** Sychar is placed either at Askar or at or near Tell Balata. The new lead is captioned as the church over the traditional Jacob's Well "near the proposed sites of Sychar". The old interior caption, "Jacob's Well (Sychar)", treated the two as one, and was reworded.
- **Berea.** The Bema is a modern memorial at a traditional spot, not an excavated ancient structure. Its kind and caption now say so.

## Observations (not changed)

- `magdala-01` is the only image left with a lead outside about 4:3 to 2.2:1 (0.90, an aerial view of the site). The curator's commit did not touch Magdala, and Commons has no clearly better wide overview of the site; most wide files are close-ups of the synagogue under its shelter. Left for a later pass.
- ADR-0029 on this branch does not yet list the `historical` kind. The schema and app gained it in `47fbecf`; the ADR text is updated on `docs/m3-feedback`.
