## Goal
Images are now the panel's main feature (ADR-0029): up to 10 per place, in four kinds, including the AI reconstructions the project will host. Show them well, without slowing the map.

## Scope
Build visual spec §3 item 1 and §11 as updated for M3.5:
1. **The panel's gallery:** up to 10 images, with arrows, the "1 / 7" counter, and a row of small thumbnails under the image when there are more than 3. The credit stays directly under the image, and the caption under the credit. The arrows are labelled "Previous image" and "Next image" (spec §7).
2. **A label on each image for its kind:** "Today", "Excavated site", "Reconstruction", or **"AI-generated reconstruction"**. The AI label is always visible and readable at 3:1 or better on any image.
3. **AI images:** they load from the site itself (`media/ai/<id>.webp` in the repository, copied into the web build). Their credit reads "AI-generated reconstruction · *tool* · *license* · Based on: *sources*", where the sources link to the research brief. Use a fixture until the first real AI image lands.
4. **A large viewer:** selecting the image opens a dialog with the image at up to 1,280 px, the same arrows, counter, label, credit and caption. ← and → move, Esc closes and returns focus, and focus stays inside the dialog.
5. **Loading:**
   - only the shown image and the next one load;
   - thumbnails in the row use Commons' smallest standard width (330 px);
   - the viewer requests 1,280 px only when it opens;
   - Commons thumbnails use only its standard widths (330, 500, 960, 1,280), since others fail;
   - no layout shift as images load;
   - a failed image shows the placeholder from spec §5, with its credit kept.
6. **Smoothness is not affected:** nothing in the gallery changes the DOM during map gestures, and the smoothness checks in `verify:web:playwright` still pass.
7. **Tests:** unit tests for kinds, credits and the loading rules; Playwright for the gallery, thumbnails, the viewer (keyboard and focus) and zero axe violations, against a 10-image fixture that includes an AI image.

Commit: `feat(panel): larger image gallery with kinds and a viewer`.
