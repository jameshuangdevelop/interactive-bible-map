## Places
Rome (`rome`), Corinth (`corinth`), Galatia (`galatia`), Ephesus (`ephesus`), Philippi (`philippi`), Colossae (`colossae`), Thessalonica (`thessalonica`), Crete (`crete`), Athens (`athens`), Antioch on the Orontes (`antioch-syria`), Caesarea Maritima (`caesarea-maritima`) and Damascus (`damascus`).

## The rule
Exactly as M3.5-02: the same three phases, brief sections, photo and prompt rules, checks and acceptance criteria. The differences:
- **The period** is about AD 45–65, Paul's time. Each brief says which buildings stood then (for example, Rome before the fire of AD 64 and long before the Colosseum, which opened in AD 80).
- **Galatia and Crete are areas:** their briefs and images show the landscape and the cities Paul's readers lived in (for Galatia, the cities in its record's history, such as Antioch in Pisidia and Iconium), and say which ones.
- **Colossae is barely excavated:** say so in its brief, and lean on the Lycus valley's landscape and on nearby Laodicea and Hierapolis only where the sources support the comparison.
