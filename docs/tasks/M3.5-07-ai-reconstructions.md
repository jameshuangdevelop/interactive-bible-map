## Goal
Publish AI reconstructions for the major places, made like a historical game's establishing shots and checked against cited research (ADR-0029 §7).

## Steps
1. **Fact-Checker, before any image is made:** read the chosen generator's current terms, and confirm that we may publish its images on a public site for anyone to reuse, including commercially. Decide the license we release them under (CC0 1.0 unless the terms or the law require otherwise), and the credit wording. Record both in `docs/LICENSES.md` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`, and finish the schema's AI fields with the GIS Engineer's rules from M3.5-01. Commit: `docs(licenses): rules for AI-generated reconstructions`.
2. **The human:** generate each prompt's image with the chosen tool, at the largest landscape size it offers. Save the files as `<id>.png` (for example `rome-ai-01.png`) in `media/ai-incoming/`, which Git ignores, and note the tool, the model and the date.
3. **Media Curator:** convert each image to WebP, at most 1,600 px wide and 400 KB, into `media/ai/<id>.webp`, and add its entry: `kind: "ai-reconstruction"`, `aiGenerated: true`, the generator, the prompt reference, the brief's source ids and a caption starting "AI-generated reconstruction of …". Put a place's overview first only if the PO agrees. Commit: `data(media): add AI reconstructions`.
4. **Fact-Checker:** check each image against its brief. Reject any image that shows something the brief rules out (for example a later building, the wrong roofs, an impossible landscape) or that has garbled details such as text or extra limbs. The human regenerates the rejected ones. Report: `docs/verification/M3.5-ai-images.md`. Commit: `docs(verification): check AI reconstructions`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The license and credit for AI images are recorded, and every AI image carries the "AI-generated reconstruction" label in the app.
- [ ] Every published AI image passed the Fact-Checker's check against its brief.
- [ ] `npm run validate:data`, `npm run check:images` and the app checks pass.

## Finish
Each agent follows the session protocol in its agent file; the Fact-Checker's last phase ends the task. PR title: `data(media): AI reconstructions for major places`.
